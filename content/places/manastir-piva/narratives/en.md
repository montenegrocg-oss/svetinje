Piva Monastery is dedicated to the Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos. Metropolitan Savatije Sokolović built the church between 1573 and 1586. Construction of a hydroelectric plant required the entire complex to be moved two kilometres from its original site in 1982; 1,260 square metres of frescoes were removed and installed again. The painting was completed in several seventeenth-century phases, and the monastery treasury preserves early books, liturgical objects, and portions of relics.
