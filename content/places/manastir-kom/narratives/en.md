## Overview {#introduction}

Đurađ and Lješ Crnojević built Kom Monastery between 1415 and 1427. The source states that it served for a time as the seat of the Metropolitanate of Zeta.

## History {#history}

A written mention is recorded in 1444. The monastery gradually lost importance after the Ottoman conquest, was almost abandoned in the eighteenth century, and was revived in the mid-nineteenth century. Conservation work was carried out in the 1980s.

## Location {#location}

The complex stands on Andrijska Gora near Žabljak Crnojevića; at high water, the rise becomes an island. The source does not provide coordinates.
