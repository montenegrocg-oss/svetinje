Podvrh Monastery stands near the source of the Bistrica River, in the wider area of Bijelo Polje. An inscription above the entrance dates the church to 1606, while its frescoes were painted in 1613/14 by Pop Strahinja of Budimlja. The icons are attributed to Jovan, known as Kozma, and his assistant Radul. The Divoš Gospel, produced around 1330, was discovered here in 1960, and the church also preserves a portion of the relics of Hieromartyr Charalambos.
