Pivska Dola Monastery is dedicated to the Third Finding of the Head of Saint John the Baptist. The church was built by relatives and descendants of the Piva martyrs as a prayerful memorial to those killed during the Second World War. It was consecrated on 7 June 2006 during the episcopate of Bishop Joanikije of Budimlja and Nikšić. The monastery is a dependency of Piva Monastery.
