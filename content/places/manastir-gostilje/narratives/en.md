Gostilje Monastery stands in the hamlet of Dobri Do, at Gostilje Martinićko in Bjelopavlići. Its church is dedicated to Venerable Mother Paraskeva—Saint Petka—whose name is also borne by the family feast of Bjelopavlići.

The monastery began as a skete of Stanjevići on donated family land. Construction on the foundations of an old house began on 13 May 2009, when the foundations of the future church were blessed.

The complex was consecrated on 3 July 2011 and established as a women’s monastery. On the day of consecration, part of a covering from Saint Petka’s relics was brought as a gift from Iași.

The complex comprises the Church of Venerable Mother Paraskeva, a residence and an auxiliary service building. Particles of the relics of several saints and part of Saint Petka’s robe from Iași are kept in the church.
