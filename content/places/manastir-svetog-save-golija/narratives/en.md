The Monastery of Saint Sava in Golija is a modern endowment built by Miodrag Daka Davidović and bequeathed to the Serbian Orthodox Church. The foundation stone for the church and monastic residence was laid in August 2005. The monastery was consecrated on 13 May 2012 with Patriarch Irinej and several bishops of the Serbian Orthodox Church taking part.
