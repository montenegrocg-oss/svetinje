The Church of Saint Thomas the Apostle stands at Bečići, close to the sea, and is one of the old Orthodox sites in this part of the Budva coast. It is dedicated to Saint Thomas, one of Christ’s Twelve Apostles.

Reliable information about the exact date of construction is scarce in the available Church sources, so its age cannot be determined precisely without further research. The church has, however, long been part of the spiritual life of Bečići and the surrounding Paštrovići area.

The veneration of Saint Stephen Štiljanović, a notable prince of Paštrovići and a saint, holds a special place in the church. A particle of his relics is kept here, and on his feast the Divine Liturgy is traditionally celebrated with the faithful gathering from Bečići and the surrounding area.

The patronal feast is Saint Thomas’s Day. The church remains an important centre of liturgical life for the Bečići parish and is also connected with the tradition of an Epiphany procession to the sea and swimming for the Holy Cross.
