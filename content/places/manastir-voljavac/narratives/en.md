Voljavac Monastery stands where the Bistrica enters the Lim and is dedicated to the Nativity of the Most Holy Theotokos. The source attributes it to Grand Župan Stefan Nemanja in the twelfth century. The church was destroyed in the eighteenth century and its stone reused for a bridge over the Bistrica. A chapel of the Holy Myrrh-bearing Women occupies the newer monastic residence. The monastery is also known in the literature as Bogorodica Bistrička.
