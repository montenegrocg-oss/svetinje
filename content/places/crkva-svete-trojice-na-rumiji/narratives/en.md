## About the church {#introduction}

According to Church tradition, a church or monastery dedicated to the Holy Trinity stood here as early as the Byzantine period. Historical sources from the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries record the tradition of an old church on Rumija, together with the centuries-old custom of carrying the Cross of Saint John Vladimir to the summit at Pentecost. According to the tradition preserved by the Metropolitanate of Montenegro and the Littoral, the old church was destroyed in the sixteenth century, at the time of the Ottoman conquest of Bar in 1571. Holy Trinity Church was restored in 2005. Its metal structure was made in two sections and, because the summit of Rumija is difficult to reach, carried there by helicopter. Metropolitan Amfilohije of Montenegro and the Littoral consecrated the restored church on 31 July 2005 and celebrated the hierarchical Divine Liturgy together with Bishop Grigorije of Zahumlje and Herzegovina.
