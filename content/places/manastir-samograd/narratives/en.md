Samograd is a monastic site dedicated to Saint Panteleimon the Great Martyr and Healer. It preserves the remains of two churches: one linked by the source to the early Christian period and another built in the Morava architectural style. The roughly 1.2-hectare site occupies three terraces and is encircled by seven tall rock formations, with traces of walls between them. The page cautiously reports that the complex was plundered and burned in the mid-fifteenth century.
