Holy Trinity Church stands in the heart of Budva’s Old Town. Built in 1804, it has been one of the centres of Orthodox spiritual life in Budva for more than two centuries. Its 220th anniversary was marked solemnly in 2024.

The church is dedicated to the Holy Trinity, and its patronal feast is celebrated at Pentecost, the Descent of the Holy Spirit upon the Apostles. Pentecost is also the feast of the city of Budva, and the church has consequently become the focal point of the city’s celebration.

The traditional Pentecost observance includes the Divine Liturgy and a solemn procession through the streets of the Old Town. The faithful come from Budva and surrounding areas. A custom is also preserved in which participants from Paštrovići, Grbalj and Miholjska Prevlaka travel by boat to Budva harbour and join the festive procession there.

Holy Trinity Church continues to play an important role in the life of Budva’s Orthodox parishes. Services are held regularly on major Church feasts, while the Pentecost celebrations bring together liturgical, spiritual and cultural events in the Old Town.

Its position within Budva’s ancient walls and its continuous liturgical life for more than two centuries make Holy Trinity Church one of the most important Orthodox holy places of old Budva.
