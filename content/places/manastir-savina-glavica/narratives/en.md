Savina Glavica Monastery stands at Glavati in Grbalj. According to tradition, a monastery with a Church of Saint Sava existed here from early times. The precise date of the older church is unknown, but an inscription above the iconostasis associated with the eighteenth century points to an earlier liturgical tradition.

The present Church of Saint Sava was built in 1874 on the site of the previous church. It served as the principal church for Krimovica, Zagora, Kovači, Glavati and Višnjevo. Since Saint Sava’s Day in 2010, the church and residence have had monastic status.

The complex comprises the Church of Saint Sava and a new residence. The church keeps particles of the relics of martyrs killed at the Lavra of Saint Sava the Sanctified and of the Glamoč Martyrs, as well as particles of the relics of Saint Anthony the Great, the Apostle Luke, Saint Seraphim of Sarov and Elder Joseph the Hesychast. Objects listed at the monastery include a vestment associated with Saint Peter of Cetinje’s services in Grbalj and a monastic head covering of Seraphim Rose.
