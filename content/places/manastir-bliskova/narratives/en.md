Bliškova Monastery, dedicated to the Entry of the Most Holy Theotokos, is located at Manastirovina in the village of Bliškovo. An early thirteenth- or fourteenth-century date has not been confirmed archaeologically; the building technique points more plausibly to the late sixteenth century. A Menaion copied in 1615 and an inscription naming Abbot Makarije attest to monastic life in the early seventeenth century. Tradition attributes the foundation to Prince Vukan, while the date of destruction remains unknown.
