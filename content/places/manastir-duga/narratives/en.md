Duga Monastery stands in the Morača canyon, on the right bank of the river along the road from Podgorica to Kolašin. It is dedicated to the Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos. Tradition attributes its foundation to Vukan Nemanjić, though no direct medieval confirmation of that claim is cited.

The monastery originally stood at Gospođino Polje, near the confluence of the Mala Rijeka and Morača. In 1752 the Kuči, Bratonožići, Vasojevići and Piperi moved it to a more secluded site in the village of Duga. Tradition says the stones were passed from hand to hand in a single night. By 1755, Bishops Sava and Vasilije Petrović had given liturgical books to the monastery. Duga served as a place of assembly and education for neighbouring tribes.

Archimandrite Mojsije Zečević, an associate of Saint Peter of Cetinje, began his monastic path here. Marko Miljanov lived at Duga with his family from 1862 to 1875. In the early twentieth century, Saint Simeon of Dajbabe painted an icon of Saint Nicholas and the sanctuary fresco of the Theotokos “More Spacious than the Heavens”. For a time, Duga was a dependency of Dajbabe.

The monastery was abandoned in 1942, and the 1979 earthquake left it in ruins. Restoration began in 1997. The 1755 church is a single-nave building with a semicircular apse, bell gable and eighteenth-century frescoes; foundations of an older church are visible in the sanctuary. The restored and enlarged residence was consecrated in 1998, and conservation of the frescoes and floor was completed in 2013.
