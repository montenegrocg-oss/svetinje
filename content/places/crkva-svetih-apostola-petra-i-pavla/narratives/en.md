The Church of the Holy Apostles Peter and Paul is situated in Risan, in the Bay of Kotor. Church sources describe it as the parish church of Risan and an important place of regular liturgical and community life for Orthodox Christians in the area.
The feast of Saints Peter and Paul, known locally as Petrovdan, is celebrated here with particular solemnity. The Divine Liturgy is followed by a festive procession through the streets of Risan.
The church is also connected with other long-standing liturgical traditions of the town. On Lazarus Saturday, a procession begins at the church and proceeds to the nearby Banja Monastery, reflecting the church’s important role in the spiritual life of Risan and its surroundings.
Today, the Church of the Holy Apostles Peter and Paul remains one of the main Orthodox places of worship in Risan, gathering the faithful for Sunday and feast-day services.
