The Monastery of Righteous Matrona of Moscow stands at Bjeloševići, a hamlet of Kovači in Lower Grbalj. It was built on land with an old house donated to the Church by Dušan Bajković and his sons Jovo and Danilo.

Construction began in 2006 with the blessing of the Intercession Monastery in Moscow, where Saint Matrona’s relics are kept. Metropolitan Amfilohije consecrated the monastery on 6 July 2008.

The residence contains a chapel of the Prophet Amos and the Martyr Vitus. It preserves the memory of the former women’s Monastery of the Prophet Amos at Zagora in Lower Grbalj. The chapel’s frescoes were consecrated on Vidovdan in 2011, and both the residence and chapel were designed in a Byzantine-Russian spirit.

The monastery keeps particles of the relics of the Holy Unmercenaries Cosmas and Damian, the Prevlaka Martyrs, Saint Luke of Crimea and Saint Theodore Ushakov.
