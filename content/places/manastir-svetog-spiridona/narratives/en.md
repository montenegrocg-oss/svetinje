The Monastery of Saint Spyridon the Wonderworker stands at Ograđenica on Paštrovska Gora, about 600 metres above sea level. It lies above Rustovo Monastery in the mountain hinterland of Paštrovići and belongs to the many holy places for which the area is often called the Holy Mountain of Paštrovići.

Little reliable information survives about the monastery’s earliest history. An older Church of Saint Spyridon is known to have stood elsewhere at Ograđenica. During Austrian rule, its site was designated for a military building, and the population was offered a new location for a church.

The present Church of Saint Spyridon was built between 1902 and 1906. It was consecrated by Danilo Ugrinović, abbot of Praskvica Monastery, Hieromonk Ilarion Bečić and Priest Savo Rađenović. It is among the larger old Paštrovići churches in the mountain hinterland of the coast.

The church again suffered severe damage in the destructive earthquake of 1979. Restoration began in early 2002 together with construction of a residence. Work ended in 2004, and the restored church and monastery were consecrated on 25 December, the feast of Saint Spyridon the Wonderworker.

Remains of an old Church of the Holy King Stefan of Dečani also lay near the monastery. The ruins were cleared, and a foundation stone for its restoration was laid on 29 September 2009.
