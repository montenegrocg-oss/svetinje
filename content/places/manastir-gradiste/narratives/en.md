## Overview {#introduction}

Gradište Monastery’s main church is dedicated to Saint Nicholas. The tradition cited by the source associates its beginnings with the early twelfth century, while a charter of 1305 is given as its written mention.

## History {#history}

The main church was restored in the early seventeenth century. The monastery suffered in 1785, lost its bells in the First World War, and was damaged and burned during the Second World War.

## Location {#location}

The complex stands on a rise above Buljarica, near Petrovac, overlooking the sea. The source does not provide coordinates.
