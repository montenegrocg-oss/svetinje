The Church of Saint Sava is situated in Rijeka Reževića, in the Paštrovići region between Sveti Stefan and Petrovac.
This area has a long Orthodox tradition and is home to numerous historic churches and monasteries. Reževići Monastery, one of the important monasteries of the Paštrovići, is located nearby.
Today, the Church of Saint Sava remains a place of worship and gathering for the faithful of Rijeka Reževića and the surrounding area.
