Boan Kadića Monastery is dedicated to the Great Martyr Demetrius. It was built on the old homestead of Todor Kadić as an endowment of the Kadić brotherhood and a place of prayerful remembrance and reconciliation.

Before construction, members of surrounding brotherhoods began cutting a road to the site. Descendants of the Kadić and related families joined in building the church and associated structures. On 28 February 2016, the brotherhood transferred the endowment to the Metropolitanate of Montenegro and the Littoral by formal document, and the complex was established as a women’s monastery that year.

Besides the Church of Saint Demetrius, the complex includes a residence, a tower guest house and an amphitheatre intended for cultural gatherings. Particles of the relics of the Martyrs of Bethlehem are kept in the church.
