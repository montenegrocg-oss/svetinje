Prekobrđe Monastery stands in the Morača region and is dedicated to the Holy Archangels. Construction began in 1996 through the efforts of Milan Vujisić, as an endowment for the repose of his son Zoran, who is buried in the church.

The site is also connected with the memory of Duke Bogić Moračanin, who died in 1487 and is regarded as the ancestor of many Morača brotherhoods. In 2006 his remains were moved from the old Zasjedica cemetery into a marble sarcophagus in the Church of the Holy Archangels. The sculptor Milan Rakočević made the sarcophagus.

A large residence has been built beside the monastery church.
