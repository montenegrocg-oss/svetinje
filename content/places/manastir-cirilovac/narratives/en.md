Ćirilovac Monastery stands at Rečine near Kolašin and is dedicated to Saints Cyril and Methodius, Equals-to-the-Apostles. Construction began in 1995 on land donated to the Church, following the blessing of Metropolitan Amfilohije.

Folk tradition associates Ćirilovac with the passage of Saint Cyril and his disciples in the ninth century on their mission among the Slavs. This motif belongs to local tradition and is not presented as a documented fact.

The new monastery was consecrated on 19 September 1999. Its church, with three bells, was built in a traditional style modelled on the churches of Studenica. The two-storey residence was consecrated on the same occasion.
