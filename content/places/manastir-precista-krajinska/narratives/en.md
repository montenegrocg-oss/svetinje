Prečista Krajinska Monastery stands on the shore of Lake Skadar near Ostros. Its main church is dedicated to the Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos, and the complex is known for its important role in the history of the Metropolitanate of Zeta.

As the metropolitan seat moved from Miholjska Prevlaka towards Cetinje, Prečista Krajinska was one of the places where the Metropolitans of Zeta resided. The monastery is mentioned in a 1417 charter of Balša III; Metropolitan Arsenije is thought to have stayed there at the time. It also appears in an Ottoman register of 1582.

The monastery is believed to have been destroyed and abandoned in the late seventeenth century during Suleiman Pasha Bushati’s campaign. Its restoration has not been completed, and services have been celebrated on the church foundations in recent times.

The complex comprises the remains of the Church of the Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos, residences and auxiliary buildings.
