Uroševica Monastery is dedicated to Saint Michael the Archangel. The church occupies an eastern slope, with the foundations of a monastic residence and traces of a straight defensive wall nearby. The complex is now overgrown by woodland. The source presents only as a hypothesis that the monastery was an endowment of King Uroš I and took its name from him.
