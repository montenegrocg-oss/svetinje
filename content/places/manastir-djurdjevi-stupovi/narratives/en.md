Đurđevi Stupovi Monastery near Berane is dedicated to Saint George the Great Martyr. The source attributes its foundation in 1213 to Župan Stefan Prvoslav, whose tomb is in the nave. Saint Sava established the Budimlja bishopric here in 1219. The complex records several building phases, while the surviving frescoes belong to different layers of painting. The monastery was burned repeatedly, most recently in 1912, and restored in 1925. The diocesan page states that it has served as the seat of the Diocese of Budimlja and Nikšić since 2001.
