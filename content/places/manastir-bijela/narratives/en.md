Bijela Monastery is dedicated to Saint George. Folk tradition attributes its eleventh-century foundation to Saint Jovan Vladimir and Prince Vulović, while the first written reference dates from 1656. The monastery was burned in 1702 and restored in 1887. It became deserted during and after the Second World War and its residence collapsed; a later renewal included the construction of a new monastic residence.
