The Lavra of Saint Simeon the Myrrh-streamer stands in Podgorica’s Old Town, where the Ribnica meets the Morača, in an area that Church tradition associates with Nemanja’s Town. According to the accounts of Saint Sava and Stefan the First-Crowned, Stefan Nemanja, later Saint Simeon the Myrrh-streamer, was born in this area in 1114.

During the Ottoman period the site became the Depedogen fortress. The renewal of Church life at Nemanja’s Town began in recent times. According to the Metropolitanate, the restored monastic complex developed around the remains of a church thought most probably to date from the fifteenth century.

The lavra’s catacomb church and spiritual centre were consecrated on 21 February 2015. On 26 February 2016, Metropolitan Amfilohije of Montenegro and the Littoral consecrated the Monastery-Lavra of Saint Simeon the Myrrh-streamer and celebrated the hierarchical Divine Liturgy there. The complex also includes the House of Patriarch Gavrilo Dožić.
