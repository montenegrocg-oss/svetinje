Zagrađe Monastery is dedicated to Saint John the Baptist and dates from the fifteenth century. The diocesan page identifies it as an endowment of Duke Stefan Vukčić Kosača. The remains of Soko Grad stand on the hill above the monastery, while the foundations of the church known as Šćepanica survive in Šćepan Polje; that church was dedicated to Saint Stephen the Archdeacon and is associated with Sandalj Hranić.
