The Monastery of Saints Cyril and Methodius—Ćirilovac stands at Rečine near Kolašin, in the mountain country beneath Bjelasica. It is dedicated to the Holy Brothers Cyril and Methodius, Equals-to-the-Apostles and enlighteners of the Slavs, and is the first monastery in the Metropolitanate of Montenegro and the Littoral dedicated to these saints.

The monastery was created in recent times through the efforts of Schema-Archimandrite Joil Bulatović. In the mid-1990s, with the blessing of Metropolitan Amfilohije of Montenegro and the Littoral, he began building the complex on his family property. His brother Janko and numerous donors also took part.

According to local tradition preserved by the monastery, Saint Cyril and his disciples passed through the region in the ninth century during their mission among the Slavic peoples. This tradition was one reason for dedicating the new holy place to Saints Cyril and Methodius.

Metropolitan Amfilohije consecrated the monastery on 19 September 1999. Its church was built in a traditional style modelled on the medieval churches of Studenica and has three bells. Organised monastic life began at Ćirilovac after the consecration, and the monastery gradually became a recognised spiritual centre of the Kolašin region.

The founding of other holy places nearby is also connected with Ćirilovac’s development. In 2005, a Church of the Transfiguration modelled on the former Lovćen chapel was consecrated at Ključki Tavor on the summit of Bjelasica. In 2007, the Monastery of the Jerusalem Mother of God was founded close to Ćirilovac.
