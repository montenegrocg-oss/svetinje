The monastery in Župa Nikšićka is dedicated to the Holy Apostle Luke. An old tradition attributes its thirteenth-century foundation to a granddaughter of Vukan Nemanjić, while fuller evidence of monastic life comes from the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. The monastery suffered destruction in 1853 and was restored by Prince Nikola in 1881 as a memorial foundation for his uncle, Prince Danilo.
