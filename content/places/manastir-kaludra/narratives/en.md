Kaludra Monastery is dedicated to the Holy Apostle Luke. The diocesan page places its foundation in the Nemanjić period and its abandonment in the mid-eighteenth century. In 2000 and 2001 the church was restored and frescoed, and a new residential building was erected. The renewed monastery was consecrated on 28 October 2001 with Patriarch Pavle and several bishops present.
