The Monastery of Saint Sergius of Radonezh stands at Mikulići beneath Mount Rumija near Bar. It was built in honour of the great Russian saint, founder of the Trinity Lavra of Saint Sergius and one of the most venerated saints of the Orthodox world.

The monastery arose in recent times below Rumija, whose summit is also marked by a church dedicated to the Holy Trinity. Donations from Orthodox believers in Russia and from Bar and the surrounding area made its construction possible. Vitaly Ivanchikov of Novosibirsk and Velimir Milo Dragović of Bar made particular contributions.

The new monastery was consecrated on 19 July 2009 by Metropolitan Amfilohije of Montenegro and the Littoral, with clergy and monastics of the Metropolitanate and the Moscow Patriarchate taking part. The hierarchical Divine Liturgy was celebrated on that occasion.

The establishment of this holy place beneath Rumija created a visible spiritual connection with the tradition of Saint Sergius and his Trinity Lavra, as well as with the long Orthodox heritage of the Bar region.
