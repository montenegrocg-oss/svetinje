Saint Paraskeva Monastery stands on Stol hill, close to the parish church at Bunovići. The holy place is also popularly known as Saint Petka and Saint Thecla, and its setting overlooks the Bay of Kotor.

The dating of the small votive church is not fully agreed. One description places it in the seventeenth century, while some art historians date it to around 1400. A restoration in 1878 is reliably recorded. The bell tower was demolished during the Austro-Hungarian occupation in 1917; the church was restored and the bell tower rebuilt in 2008.

A two-storey residence of dressed stone with a tiled roof and auxiliary buildings were constructed beside the church. They created the conditions needed to establish a monastic community.
