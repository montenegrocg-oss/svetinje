The Monastery of the Great Martyr George—Kosmač stands on the island of the same name in the north-western part of Lake Skadar, in an area known as Gornje Malo Blato. Laurel covers the island, and the monastery was probably once connected with the nearby holy places of Sinjac and Goljemadi in the Lješanska nahija.

Folk tradition associates the monastery’s origin with the fourteenth century and King Vukašin Mrnjavčević. It says that Vukašin married Jevrosima, the mother of Prince Marko, at Kosmač, and an old wedding custom is consequently linked with the site. In the sources, however, the historical connection between Kosmač and the Mrnjavčević dynasty is preserved primarily as tradition.

The monastery was abandoned in the first half of the sixteenth century and restored in the early eighteenth. At the end of that century it was again destroyed during an Ottoman military campaign against Montenegro. It later became a dependency of Cetinje Monastery and functioned during the nineteenth century, but in the twentieth it again lost its monastic life and gradually fell into ruin.

The main Church of Saint George is a single-nave stone building with a narthex. Study of the surviving walls shows that it was built in at least two phases. The older part, with a nave and semicircular apse, most probably dates from the fourteenth century; the church was extended with a narthex during the eighteenth-century restoration. It had a barrel vault and was probably roofed with stone slabs.

Work to revive the ancient holy place began in the early twenty-first century. Clearing of the overgrown complex intensified from 2002, while restoration began directly in 2004 and gained momentum two years later. Metropolitan Amfilohije of Montenegro and the Littoral consecrated the restored Church of the Great Martyr George on 3 May 2009, beginning a new period in the monastery’s life.

The church was rebuilt in dressed stone and its interior painted with frescoes. A bell tower was erected on the plateau west of the church. Today Saint George’s Monastery at Kosmač is a dependency of Cetinje Monastery and one of the restored old holy places of Lake Skadar.
