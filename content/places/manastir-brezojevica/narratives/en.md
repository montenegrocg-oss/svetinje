Brezojevica Monastery is dedicated to the Holy Trinity. Folk tradition links it to the early medieval and Nemanjić periods, while the source presents a possible former dependency on Visoki Dečani or the Patriarchate of Peć only as a hypothesis. The monastery was deserted from 1850 and suffered destruction in 1912 and after 1945. The church then served as a parish cemetery church before monastic life was renewed.
