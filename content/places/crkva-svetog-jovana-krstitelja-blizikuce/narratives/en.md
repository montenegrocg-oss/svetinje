The Church of the Nativity of Saint John the Baptist stands in the Paštrovići village of Blizikuće, in the hills above Sveti Stefan.
The church remains an active place of worship. Its principal annual celebration is the feast of the Nativity of Saint John the Baptist, when the Divine Liturgy is celebrated and the faithful from Blizikuće and the surrounding Paštrovići villages gather together.
The church stands within one of the historic Orthodox areas of Paštrovići, known for its many churches and monasteries, and it continues to serve as a place of prayer and local gathering.
