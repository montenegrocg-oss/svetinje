Pelev Brijeg Monastery stands beneath Mount Vjeternik, north-east of Podgorica, at an old assembly place of the Bratonožići. Its church is dedicated to Saint Nicholas.

Folk tradition speaks of an early Christian catacomb monastery and a renewal in the Nemanjić period, but written evidence for the earliest phase has not survived. Historical information records priests serving at the church from the mid-fifteenth century, after which sources are sparse until the late eighteenth and early nineteenth centuries.

Ilija Braković bequeathed a house with a large library to the monastery. The house was later demolished and the library dispersed and destroyed. The church itself was damaged so badly that services could not be held.

Restoration began in the late twentieth century, and Metropolitan Amfilohije consecrated the restored Church of Saint Nicholas on 21 June 2003. The complex comprises the church and a residence formed from two small wooden buildings.
