## Overview {#introduction}

Donje Brčele Monastery has a church dedicated to Saint Nicholas. The literature summarised by the source most often dates its construction to the fourteenth or fifteenth century, while folk tradition associates its foundation with Jelena Balšić in the early fifteenth century.

## History {#history}

The source describes the monastery as a spiritual, political, cultural and educational centre of Crmnica. The first eight-year village school was founded here, and the church was restored in 1861.

## Location {#location}

The source places the monastery at Donje Brčele but does not provide geographical coordinates.
