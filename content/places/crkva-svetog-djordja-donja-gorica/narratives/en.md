The Church of Saint George stands in the Podgorica neighbourhood of Donja Gorica and is a long-established place of Orthodox worship in the area. Reliable information about its original construction has not survived in the available Church sources, so its earliest history cannot be dated precisely.

The church is dedicated to the Great Martyr George, and its patronal feast is Đurđic, the feast of the Dedication of the Church of Saint George. Regular liturgical gatherings and celebrations of the patronal feast testify to the importance of this holy place in the life of Donja Gorica.

The church and its complex underwent a substantial renewal in recent times. The fresco painting of the interior was completed by 2015, and the associated buildings were improved in the following years. A new guest house was consecrated in 2016, continuing the development of the area around the church.

The Church of Saint George remains an important place of Orthodox life in Donja Gorica, gathering the faithful especially on Đurđic and the great feasts of the Church.
