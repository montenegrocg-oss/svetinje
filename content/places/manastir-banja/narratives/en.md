Banja Monastery stands close to the shore between Risan and Perast. Folk tradition connects its name with Roman baths that disappeared in an earthquake together with part of ancient Risan.

Monastery tradition attributes its foundation to Grand Župan Stefan Nemanja, later Saint Simeon the Myrrh-streamer. The church is dedicated to the Great Martyr George. A tradition concerning a particle of Saint George’s relics is also connected with the Nemanjić period: it is believed that Stefan the First-Crowned gave it to the monastery after Saint Sava brought it from Nicaea.

The monastery was destroyed in 1654 during the Battle of Perast. The relics were moved to Kosijerevo, then to Tvrdoš and Savina, before being returned to Banja in 1913 through the efforts of Archimandrite Dionisije.

Because the site is confined, the complex is arranged in terraces on two levels. The Church of Saint George and the cemetery occupy the upper plateau, with the monastic residences below.
