Kosijerevo Monastery, dedicated to the Nativity of the Most Holy Theotokos, dates from the first half of the fourteenth century. After its destruction in 1807 it was restored in 1817 through the efforts of Hadži Dionisije Dobrićevac. Construction of a reservoir led to the relocation of the complex from the left bank of the Trebišnjica to Petrovići in Banjani in 1979. The relics of Saint Arsenije Sremac remained here from 1884 to 1920; the source states that the monastery now preserves the foot of the Holy Apostle Luke.
