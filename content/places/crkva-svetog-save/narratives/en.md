The Church of Saint Sava stands in the village of Đenaši, in the Paštrovići region above Sveti Stefan. It forms part of the historic Orthodox landscape of Paštrovići, an area rich in churches and monasteries.
The Metropolitanate of Montenegro and the Littoral lists the Church of Saint Sava in Đenaši among the churches in Montenegro dedicated to Saint Sava. Divine Liturgy continues to be celebrated there, together with feast days associated with Saint Sava and his family.
The feast of Saint Anastasia of Serbia, the mother of Saint Sava, is also celebrated at the church, bringing together the faithful from Paštrovići and the surrounding area.
Today, the Church of Saint Sava remains an active place of worship and one of the recognizable Orthodox sanctuaries on the slopes above Sveti Stefan.
