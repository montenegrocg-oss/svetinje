Šudikovo Monastery, dedicated to the Entry of the Most Holy Theotokos, stands on the right bank of the Lim at the entrance to the Tifran Gorge. The diocesan page describes it as an important centre of teaching, manuscript copying, and church art. Sixteenth- and seventeenth-century manuscripts and the work of the painter Strahinja of Budimlja are associated with it. The complex was destroyed in 1738. The proposed derivation of its name from a church court and stories of healing at the Holy Spring remain explicitly hypotheses and traditions.
