Dobrilovina Monastery is dedicated to Saint George. The diocesan page calls it a Nemanjić endowment and records the popular name Little Morača, reflecting its resemblance to Morača Monastery. It is mentioned in the sixteenth century and was restored in 1609. The monastery became deserted after the Second World War, while the church was restored and consecrated again in 1989.
