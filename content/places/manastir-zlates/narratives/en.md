Zlateš Monastery was formed when the old parish church of the Holy Prophet Elijah in the village of Tomaševo received monastic status. Its name preserves an earlier name of the Vraneš valley. The diocesan page places this within the broader development of the Lim region during the Nemanjić period and the role of its churches and monasteries. The story explaining how the name Zlateš gave way to Vraneš is explicitly retained as local tradition rather than established history.
