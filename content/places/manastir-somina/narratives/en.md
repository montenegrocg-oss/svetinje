Somina Monastery stands at Hercegove Luke and is dedicated to the Transfiguration of the Lord. Construction began in 1996 as an endowment of Radomir Lučić with the support of other donors. The completed church was consecrated on 9 October 2001. Somina is a dependency of Kosijerevo Monastery.
