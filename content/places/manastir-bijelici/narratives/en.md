The monastery church is a single-nave stone building of the seventeenth century, aligned east–west on Bijelići hill above Soliolsko Polje. It originally served as a village parish church and later became part of a monastery complex. It is dedicated to the Miracle of the Archangel Michael at Chonae.

The interior is painted with frescoes and the chandelier was made in Russia. A fresco of the Archangel Michael appears above the west entrance, while a bell gable with one bell crowns the wall. The north and south walls each have one window.

The monastery courtyard contains a traditional threshing floor that also serves as a viewpoint over the Monastery of the Holy Archangel Michael on the Island of Flowers. Mosaics of four saints adorn the inner side of the monastery wall.
