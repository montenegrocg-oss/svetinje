Vojnić Monastery stands in the mountain hinterland of Paštrovići, in the village of Vojnići near Duljevo. Its main church is dedicated to the Great Martyr Demetrius, and the holy place is therefore also popularly known as the Monastery of Saint Mitar.

Neither its foundation nor the beginning of its abandonment is documented. It is thought to be older than nearby Duljevo, while folk tradition associates its history and the origin of the Vojnić brotherhood with the fifteenth century. The proximity of Duljevo and related stylistic features in the churches indicate connections between the two complexes, but their exact nature is not established.

After a long period of abandonment, the churches collapsed and became overgrown, while the 1979 earthquake destroyed the remaining walls. Restoration began in 2004. The renewed complex comprises the churches of Saint Demetrius and Saint Nicholas and a residence.

The monastery keeps particles of the relics of Saint Seraphim of Sarov, the Prevlaka Martyrs and Saints Peter and Fevronia of Murom.
